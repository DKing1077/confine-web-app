You found me under flicker-light and rain,
Half a joke and half a runaway train.
Your smile cut through the noise like summer glass,
Turning every red light green as we passed.

We talked in sparks, in almosts, in maybe,
Like the night itself was trying to save me.
Your hand brushed mine, a quiet little dare,
And suddenly the dark felt less like fear.

The city leaned in close to hear us breathe,
Neon halos hanging from your sleeve.
I said I’m fine, the oldest lie I know,
You said stay soft, don’t disappear below.

So we kept walking past the last train sign,
Trading broken futures for borrowed time.
If morning comes and takes this dream apart,
Leave your echo like a key inside my heart.
